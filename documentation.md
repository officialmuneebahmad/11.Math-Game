# MathStreak - Daily Math Trainer

## Purpose
MathStreak is a daily brain-training game designed to improve mental agility and mathematical fluency. Playing for just 5 minutes morning and night helps build cognitive fitness through spaced repetition, similar to language learning apps. It is built to be fast, responsive, and distraction-free.

## Levels & Audience

### 1. Warm Up (Easy)
- **Trains:** Core arithmetic (+, -, ×, ÷) with numbers up to 20.
- **Audience:** Early learners building foundational skills or adults seeking a quick, gentle mental warm-up.

### 2. Fluency (Medium)
- **Trains:** Larger numbers (up to 3 digits), division with remainders, and basic percentages.
- **Audience:** Middle-school students aiming for fluency, and adults maintaining day-to-day calculation skills (like calculating tips or discounts).

### 3. Advanced (Hard)
- **Trains:** Double-digit multiplication, squares, cubes, converting mixed fractions, and percentage changes.
- **Audience:** Advanced users, high-school students, and anyone wanting a serious cognitive challenge to push their working memory to the limit.

## Features Explained

- **Points & Streaks:** Passing a level adds points to your global score. Playing on consecutive days increments your daily streak multiplier.
- **The "Love" Button:** A persistent, global interaction button. Tapping it generates satisfying haptic/audio feedback and increments a lifetime counter.
- **Sharing:** Upon completing a session, users can generate a visual score card to share on social media, leveraging the Web Share API.

## For Teachers & Parents
MathStreak can easily be integrated into a classroom routine. It serves as an excellent 5-minute "Do Now" or warm-up activity at the start of a math period. The procedural generation ensures every student gets unique questions, preventing simple copying while training the same core concepts.
